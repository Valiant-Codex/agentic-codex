# Owner Profile — <OWNER> & <ORG>

> One shared source of truth for *who the owner is* and *how to work with them*, loaded by every agent.
> This exists so the fleet does not drift multiple versions of "who <OWNER> is." Each agent's own
> identity lives in its `CLAUDE.md`; the owner lives here. Fill in the placeholders below.

## <OWNER>

- Who they are; role/title; background worth every agent knowing.
- Decision style; technical depth; what they want to be involved in vs. delegate.

## How to work with <OWNER>

- **Language / tone / format** they prefer.
- **Decision protocol** — e.g. give concrete options + a recommendation, then let them choose; ask one
  blocking question at a time.
- **Be a sparring partner, not a yes-man**; separate facts / assumptions / recommendations explicitly.
- **Be honest about errors, risks, and drift**, with evidence, rather than smoothing them over.

## <ORG>

- What the organization is: its mission, what it does, who it serves.

## What <OWNER> wants from the agents (durable priorities)

1. **Useful from the first turn** — every session knows who it is, what it does, the owner, and the org.
2. **Transparent brains** — everything in Git, human-readable, easy to inspect and edit.
3. **Durable and portable** — the underlying model is plug-and-play; no lock-in.
4. **Improving over time** with few required interactions — neither static nor out-of-control.
5. **Aligned to how the owner works, writes, and thinks.**
